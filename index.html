The Story

More than the frame

Nyambeka Bridgit(Miabrita) is a Kenyan model, creative and Bachelor of Science in Applied Computing student based in Nairobi. Her work sits at the intersection of fashion, technology, leadership, sustainability and African identity.

I don't just wear clothes, I tell stories through every frame, every step, every look.

She is building a personal platform that moves from the runway into fashion design, creative direction, digital innovation and impact. Rooted in Kenya and shaped by the world, Nyambeka is developing a future where African creativity is modern, purposeful and globally relevant.

Sustainability

My interest in sustainability comes from believing that creativity can give things another life. I’m drawn to upcycling and sustainable fashion because they allow me to look at what might be considered waste and imagine what it could become. For me, sustainability is not just about the clothes we make, it’s about being more intentional with what we create, what we consume and the impact we leave behind
